## FAQ
### What's the difference between coaching and therapy?
Coaching is forward-facing, practice-based work — building habits, clarifying decisions, working through specific goals. Therapy is clinical work for diagnosable conditions and deeper psychological care. Our coaches are not licensed therapists, and we'll always tell you when something is outside coaching's lane.

### How are coaches vetted?
Every coach completes an application that includes credentials, experience, and a brief about how they work. We review each application and admit coaches who meet our standards for their dimension. We're small enough right now that this is a person-to-person review, not an algorithm.

### What does a session cost?
Coaches set their own rates. You'll see the rate clearly on each coach's profile before you book. There are no membership fees and no hidden charges — you pay per session, and that's it.

### Can I work with more than one coach at a time?
Yes — and many clients do. A financial coach and a spiritual director in the same season is a common combination. The four dimensions are connected, and your support can be too.

## 4) Accessibility & UX Standards

- Preserve visible focus states for keyboard navigation.
- Keep decorative artwork hidden from assistive technology.
- Provide meaningful alt text for informative images; use empty alt (`alt=""`) for decorative images.
- Preserve reduced-motion support for users with motion sensitivity.
- Keep color contrast at AA-compliant levels.
- Keep clear CTA labels and tap-friendly targets.
